Affinity Mapping Activity

In this activity, you’ll group insights from the PME scenario into four themes: Content Relevance, Engagement & Delivery, System Constraints, and Motivation & Emotion.

Drag each sticky note from the top into the category that best fits. On smaller screens, the cards will appear in a single stack instead of side-by-side.

Tip: There is no single “perfect” answer. Focus on grouping cards by underlying meaning rather than exact wording.

1 Content Relevance
2 Engagement & Delivery
3 System Constraints
4 Motivation & Emotion

Reflection

After you finish sorting, take a screenshot or print this page and submit to your instructor. Then answer: What patterns did you notice? Which category surprised you the most?
