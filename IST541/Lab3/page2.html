Brainstorming Activity

During this short brainstorming activity, we will practice the first phase of Brainstorming,

We will practice divergent rapid idea generation. Take 5 minutes to generate as many ideas as you can.

Phase 1: Divergent broad idea generation. (“wild” ideas—sometimes absurdity unlocks creativity)

Phase 2: Group and cluster ideas into similar categories.

Phase 3: Convergent Phase, elimitate unfeasible, impractical ideas, and narrow the list to 2-3 ideas.
